Our Children Welfare Programs

Food Distribution Programs

We provide nutritious meals weekly to children and families in need to ensure they grow healthy and strong.

Educational Support

We provide school supplies, tutoring, and scholarships to help children achieve their educational goals.

Healthcare Assistance

We organize health checkups, vaccinations, and medical support for children from underprivileged communities.

Skill Development Programs

We teach life skills, vocational skills, and creative activities like art and music to help children explore talents and prepare for a better future.

Shelter and Safety

We offer secure housing facilities where children are protected from harm and given a stable place to live. Alongside physical shelter, our programs ensure emotional security through counseling, supervision, and care, helping children feel valued and supported as they grow.

Ongoing Projects

Summer Learning Camp

A camp that provides interactive learning, games, and workshops to keep children engaged and learning during school breaks.

Community Kitchen Project

We run weekly community kitchens to feed underprivileged children and families while educating them about nutrition and hygiene.
